# Design Review Skill

Use this skill before finalizing UI changes.

Review checklist:
- Is the app light-themed?
- Is the visual style clean, institutional, and finance-grade?
- Are spacing, borders, shadows, and colors consistent?
- Are KPI cards readable?
- Is the table dense but not cramped?
- Is the selected timeframe obvious?
- Are positive, caution, and defensive states visually distinct?
- Does the design work on mobile with horizontal table scrolling?
